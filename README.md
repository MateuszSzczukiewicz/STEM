# Academic STEM Implementations

## About This Repository

This repository serves as a practical portfolio of my university coursework and personal studies in Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM). Instead of just theoretical notes, this collection contains hands-on implementations, simulations, and practical solutions to academic problems.

The primary goal is to document my "learning-by-doing" approach, demonstrating how theoretical concepts from subjects like statistics, algorithms, and electronics can be modeled, verified, and understood through code.

## Philosophy

I believe the most effective way to truly understand complex theories is to build them. This repository is a testament to that philosophy. Here, abstract formulas and concepts are translated into tangible code, whether it's a Monte Carlo simulation to verify a probability problem or a simple circuit model.

## Structure

The repository is organized by subject matter to maintain clarity and avoid becoming a "junk drawer" of random files. Each main folder corresponds to a specific academic course or field of study.
